AXIS 1942

The classic world-war strategy board game, playable on your iPad.

What it is

A faithful digital edition of the 1942 Second Edition world-war strategy board game. Command the Soviet Union, Germany, the United Kingdom, Japan, or the United States across a world map of 96 territories and 65 sea zones. Build armies, navies, and air forces; fight dice-driven battles; capture capitals; and win by controlling victory cities.

Get it on your iPad

  1. Open conikersystems.github.io/axis in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch Axis 1942 from your home screen — it runs full-screen and offline.
  4. After updates, use the in-app 🔄 Update button to get the latest version.

How a turn works

  • Purchase — spend IPCs on new units and factory repairs.
  • Combat Move — drag units into hostile territories and sea zones.
  • Conduct Combat — resolve every battle with dice.
  • Noncombat Move — reinforce and land your aircraft.
  • Mobilize — place newly purchased units at your industrial complexes.
  • Collect Income — earn IPCs for the territories you control.
